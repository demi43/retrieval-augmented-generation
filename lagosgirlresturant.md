## 1. About Us

Lagos Girl Restaurant serves home-style Nigerian and West African food with a modern twist. The restaurant was founded in 2021 by Chef Adaeze Okafor, who grew up in Surulere, Lagos, and learned to cook in her grandmother's kitchen. Our goal is to bring the taste of a Lagos Sunday lunch to our neighborhood.

We seat 48 guests indoors and 16 on our covered patio. The patio is heated from October through March.

## 4. Allergens and Dietary Information

- **Peanuts:** Suya spice (yaji) contains ground peanuts. It is used on suya skewers, chicken suya and the Asun marinade. Please tell your server about any nut allergy — we cannot guarantee a nut-free kitchen.
- **Fish and shellfish:** Egusi Soup and Efo Riro (beef version) are made with stockfish and ground crayfish. The mushroom Efo Riro is made without seafood.
- **Gluten:** Most mains are naturally gluten-free. Items containing gluten: Puff-Puff, Spring Rolls, Agege Bread French Toast, Chin Chin, and Cheesecake.
- **Dairy:** Most savory dishes are dairy-free. Desserts contain dairy.
- **Vegan options:** Veggie Jollof, Mushroom Efo Riro, Beans & Plantain, Plantain Chips, Spring Rolls, all swallows, Fried Plantain, Moin Moin (made without egg on request).
- **Halal:** Our chicken and beef are halal certified. Goat meat is sourced from a halal supplier. We serve alcohol on the premises.

## 11. Frequently Asked Questions

**Is the food very spicy?**
Spice levels vary from mild to hot. Every dish is labeled, and most dishes can be made milder on request. Pepper Soup, Ofada & Ayamase, and Asun are the spiciest and cannot be made fully mild.

**What is a "swallow"?**
A swallow is a soft, dough-like side made from yam, cassava or plantain flour. You pinch off a small piece with your fingers, roll it, and use it to scoop up soup. We are happy to provide cutlery.

**What is the difference between Jollof and Fried Rice?**
Jollof rice is cooked in a tomato and pepper base, giving it a red color and smoky flavor. Nigerian fried rice is cooked with vegetables, curry and thyme, and is yellow-green in color.

**Do you have a kids' menu?**
We don't have a separate kids' menu, but half portions of Jollof Rice, Fried Plantain and Chicken are available for $9.

**Can I bring a birthday cake?**
Yes. There is a $3 per person cake-cutting fee. We will sing happy birthday on request.

**Do you have Wi-Fi?**
Yes, free Wi-Fi is available. Ask your server for the password.

**Is there live music?**
Yes. We host live Afrobeats and highlife music every Friday from 8 PM to 10:30 PM. No cover charge.
